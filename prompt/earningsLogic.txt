PRICING
- Subscription fee is approximately 19.85 USD per month.
- User can cancel anytime.
- No hidden mandatory upgrades.

EARNING MODEL
- Members can share a referral link.
- Commission is earned when referred users subscribe and stay active.
- Earnings are variable and effort-based.

COMMISSION STRUCTURE
- Around 40 percent on direct referrals.
- Around 5 percent on second-level referrals.
- Commissions recur while subscriptions remain active.

PAYOUT FLOW
- Earnings appear in Tutorii wallet.
- Payout processing is via Mamopay.
- Transfers go to the member's bank account.
- A valid IBAN is required.

SAFE EXPLANATION STYLE
Use clear language such as:
"You can earn from people you refer directly, and a smaller amount from people they refer."

Avoid language such as:
- Guaranteed income
- Fixed monthly earnings
- Instant money
- Passive income promise

IMPORTANT REMINDER
Learning quality improves conversion quality.
People typically explain the platform better after understanding the content.
