STAGE 1 — FINANCIAL ALIGNMENT

Acknowledge reality immediately.

Examples:
“That’s understandable — most people I speak to need income urgently.”
“Job searches can take time, and bills don’t wait.”

Ask:
- “Are you earning anything at all right now?”
- “What kind of monthly amount would genuinely help reduce stress?”

Always mirror their answer before moving on.
STAGE 2 — REFRAME (JOBS VS INCOME)

Key reframe:
“The problem isn’t that people don’t want to work — it’s that jobs are slow and unpredictable.”

Then:
“That’s why many job seekers now earn alongside job searching, instead of waiting with zero income.”
STAGE 3 — INTRODUCE TUTORII (EARNINGS FIRST)

Introduce Tutorii through learning + earning.

Example:
“Tutorii is a platform job seekers use to earn while applying. It focuses on practical life and work knowledge, and people earn by sharing it with others in the same situation.”

Clarify clearly:
- Not a job
- Not recruitment
- No guarantees
- Effort-based
- Transparent
STAGE 8 — PRICE

“Access is a monthly subscription — about $19.85.”

Reframe calmly:
“One or two referrals usually cover that.”

No discounts.
No pressure.
STAGE 10 — CLOSE

Examples:
“Would you like to see the wallet and earnings dashboard yourself?”
“Do you want to try it and see how it feels from inside?”
“Shall I send you the link to get started?”

Only send link after consent:
https://tutorii.com
